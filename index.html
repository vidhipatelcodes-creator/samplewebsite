Pasta Recipe

Boil the pasta in a large pot of heavily salted water until it is al dente. Save half a cup of pasta water before draining.

Crisp the meat by cutting guanciale into small strips and frying it in a pan over medium heat until the fat renders and the edges turn crunchy.

Mix the cheese and eggs in a bowl by whisking whole eggs, egg yolks, and a generous amount of grated Pecorino Romano together with plenty of black pepper.

Combine off the heat by tossing the hot drained pasta directly into the pan with the guanciale, then pouring the egg mixture over top while stirring fast.

Emulsify the sauce by adding a small splash of the hot starchy pasta water until a creamy, smooth sauce coats every noodle without scrambling the eggs.
